About Us

Helping travellers discover, compare and plan their next adventure.

Who We Are

Destination is a travel recommendation platform built for people who love to explore. We bring together beaches, temples, countries and cities in one simple place, with images and descriptions that make it easy to decide where to go next.

Our app searches a structured collection of destinations and shows you relevant results instantly, so you spend less time searching and more time planning.
